About the Report

As artificial intelligence develops rapidly, traditional government open data must evolve toward AI-ready open data. This report examines formats, metadata, licensing, privacy protection, cross-agency coordination, and public-private collaboration to show how data stewards can make open data easier for AI tools to understand and responsibly reuse.

The report is also intended as a communication resource between government and civic communities. It helps public agencies identify practical paths for data governance, while giving advocates, civic technologists, and open-source communities concrete examples for dialogue with agencies at different stages of readiness.

Case Studies

The research presents five international cases, showing how AI-ready open data can begin under different institutional and technical conditions:

  • Spain's ImpulsaDATA: Companion-style support that helps public agencies navigate regulatory and data-release risks.
  • Germany's The German Commons: Openly licensed German-language training data with an automated processing workflow.
  • Open Development Cambodia: Technical assistance and trust-building to encourage data sharing where legal frameworks are still developing.
  • Germany's Parla: Public parliamentary documents and generative AI tools used to improve administrative search and responses to citizens.
  • France's CroissantLLM: Government open data and openly licensed corpora used to train a transparent bilingual open language model.

Key Takeaways

  1. AI-ready open data is not only a technical upgrade; it is public infrastructure that helps civil servants, citizens, and AI systems use government data more accurately.
  2. Machine-readable formats, complete metadata, and clear licensing are foundational for reducing processing costs and enabling reuse.
  3. Privacy, copyright, and bias risks should be reviewed before data enters AI training or applications.
  4. Public-private collaboration can begin with lower-sensitivity topics, concrete needs, and visible outcomes that build long-term trust.
  5. High-quality local data helps preserve linguistic and cultural context, preventing AI systems from reflecting only dominant languages and external cultural frameworks.
