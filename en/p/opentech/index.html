Through this advocacy project, OCF focuses on the infrastructure needed for open-issue advocacy: ongoing internal capacity building, tracking external developments, developing policy advocacy tools, and hosting Open Technology Meetups alongside new outputs. We also connect with public-sector, private-sector, academic, civic, and community needs to provide consultation, training, and workshop collaboration.

About Open Technology

Open Technology brings together open source, open data, and open government. It means that the construction of the digital world, the collection of data, and government governance should be carried out in open, transparent, and participatory ways. These principles help advance digital rights, internet freedom, and a more resilient digital civic society.

The Three Opens and OCF's Advocacy Work

Open Source - Technology Built Through Public Participation

Open source supports product development, industrial progress, internet freedom, and digital rights. When the production of code and technology is more diverse and less monopolized, more people can participate in building a digital society that is fair, free, and protective of users' rights. Open source should therefore be understood not only as access to source code, but also as a set of values for facing technological change.

Advocacy Directions

  1. Strengthen the open source software ecosystem.
  2. Urge government implementation of Public Money, Public Code (PMPC) to practice open technology.
  3. Encourage organizations to obtain OpenChain certification and reduce legal risks.

Open Data - A Cornerstone for Digital Transformation

Digital and internet applications have become part of daily life and government systems, expanding the possibility for individuals to participate in public decision-making. Open data is a cornerstone for that participation. Only by building accessible open data can society strengthen public participation, oversight, and government transparency. The purpose of open data should emphasize public well-being, not only economic value-added reuse.

Advocacy Directions

  1. Legalize government data openness.
  2. Promote open data that is accessible to the public and useful for public needs.

Open Government - Transparent, Efficient, Accountable Public-Private Collaboration

Since 2016, OCF has actively promoted open government. We organized Taiwan civil society delegations to attend Open Government Partnership Global Summits and published Taiwan's first civil society Open Government Observation Report in 2017. After the Executive Yuan and Legislative Yuan adopted OGP principles for action plans in 2020, OCF participated in preparation, planning discussions, and public-sector, civic, and international training and forums.

In 2024, during the proposal phase for Taiwan's second Open Government National Action Plan, OCF connected its open source, open data, and open government advocacy work. The proposal focused on gaps in the government's open data system and on building better mechanisms for citizen participation in data and decision-making.

Advocacy Directions

  1. Promote a dedicated open data law and improve Taiwan's data governance and application environment.
  2. As public agencies promote open government action plans under OGP principles, continue engaging government and civic organizations in the planning and implementation of commitments.
